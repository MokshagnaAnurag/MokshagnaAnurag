## About Me

I'm a **B.Tech Electronics & Communication Engineering** graduate from **MVGR College of Engineering**, with hands-on research and internship experience spanning **autonomous robotics, CubeSat systems, UAV platforms, and embedded intelligence**.

I build systems that operate reliably at the intersection of hardware and software — from LiDAR-based navigation stacks and ROS 2 control pipelines to RP2040-driven satellite subsystems and flight-controller integration on real UAV platforms.

- 🔭 Currently working on **autonomous UAV software** at Skyeris Aerotech
- 🛰️ Building a **modular 1U CubeSat** with IIT Tirupati's I-Hub Foundation
- 🌱 Exploring **Visual SLAM, Nav2, and physical AI** for autonomous navigation
- 📝 Published research in **IEEE, SSRN, and IJRASET**
- 💬 Ask me about **ROS 2, ArduPilot/PX4, embedded systems, or CubeSat design**
- 📫 Open to **full-time roles and internships** in robotics, space technologies, and autonomous systems
